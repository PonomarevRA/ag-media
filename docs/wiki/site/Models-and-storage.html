Model responsibilities

LayerExecutor / limitation
TextWhisper through a local backend; speech and UI locale are independent
Voice groupsSherpa, with separate identity and short-turn review modules
Faces / identityYuNet + SFace and conservative profile association
Visible speakingLight-ASD; the visible person may be silent while someone else speaks
Objects / textNanoDet and OCR backends; observed labels may vary
Body / gaze / clothingRequested local vision/MediaPipe workers
Facial expressionsVisible-expression classifier, not psychological inference
Audio / intonationPlatform backends including Apple SoundAnalysis and Zipformer
SynthesisQwen3-TTS and Chatterbox in the primary mode; previous engines are research options

This describes responsibilities, not guaranteed installation. Inspect current readiness, paths, backend and model terms in configuration and THIRD-PARTY.md. Fish may run as a separate local service. Initial setup can download weights; building the wiki installs no models.
